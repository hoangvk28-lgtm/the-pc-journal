# The PC Journal — photo shot list (real photos for E-E-A-T)

## Specs for every photo
- Your own photo (phone is fine), not a manufacturer, Amazon or stock image.
- Landscape 16:9, at least 1600 x 900 px, JPG or PNG (we convert to WebP).
- Plain, tidy background (desk, mat, case interior). Good daylight, no heavy filters, no RGB glow.
- The part's label/connector must be readable when the shot is about that label or connector.
- File name = the name in the "File" column. Send them in one folder; we put them in `public/images/pcj/`.
- Captions will describe what is shown ("A 12V-2x6 cable plugged into a graphics card"), never "tested by us".
